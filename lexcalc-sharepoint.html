Legal costs

odmena advokáta · súdny poplatok
Attorney fee
Tarifná hodnota (§ 10 vyhl. 655/2004 / § 8 vyhl. 177/1996) — normally the sum claimed. Leave blank to use the total of the enabled invoices.
Acts of legal service (úkony)
One row per act: the date it was performed, a description, how many identical acts, and the ZSTO tariff multiplier (e.g. 1/2 for a further same-day act). The date sets that act's režijný paušál year and becomes the cost origination date when pushed to the claim.
Leave blank to resolve the režijný paušál automatically from each act's year (1/100 of the výpočtový základ; CZ: 300 Kč). Enter a value to force one rate for all acts.
Tick if the attorney is a VAT payer — adds 23 % (SK) / 21 % (CZ) on top of the net fee.
